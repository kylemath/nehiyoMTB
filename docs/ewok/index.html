Why no Cree?

Most of the plaques on this wall pair an English MTB trail name with a carved Plains Cree calque — sometimes a careful translation, sometimes a riff. The Ewok plaque is one of two on the wall (along with slinky) that does not carry a Cree headline. The design source in the ewok/ folder is a hooded forest‑creature silhouette inspired by the Endor‑dwelling Ewoks of Return of the Jedi, and the trail itself appears to be a short, tight, low‑clearance forest section — the kind of trail where the rider hunches small to fit through, the way an Ewok moves through the trees of Endor.

The catalogue mirrors the plaque: creeName is the placeholder string ewok and creeSyllabics is empty. We are not inventing a Cree name here — that would put words in the carvers' hands they did not choose to carve. If a Cree name is added later (the natural cultural analogue would be something built from memekwêsiw 'little forest being'), the page can be re‑drafted and the plaque eventually re‑carved. Until then, this page stays English‑only and points at the missing piece honestly.

Like every other sign on the wall, the trail still sits on ᓀᐦᐃᔭᐤ ᐊᐢᑭᕀ Nêhiyaw‑Askiy (Plains Cree territory). The land underneath an English name is the same land — naming the missing translation is itself part of the work.
