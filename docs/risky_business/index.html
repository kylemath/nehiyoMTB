The Name & Its Meaning

The chosen Plains Cree rendering for “Risky Business” means “the dangerous work/affair.”

ayâhastêw/astêw 'it is dangerous' + atoskêwin 'work, business'.

Design & Trail

Sign icon: Risky Business movie homage — a guy slipping off the front lip of a battered 80s sports car.

Wordplay / second meaning: Film is about a teen running an illicit business; 'business' euphemism preserved by atoskêwin.

Trail stats: Located in Gold Bar/Capilano Park; about 423 m long; -18 m descent / 9 m ascent.
