The Name & Its Meaning

The chosen Plains Cree rendering for “Hustler” means “a tricky, clever outwitter.”

kakayêsi- 'cunning, sly, full of tricks' as an actor noun: a slick rider who uses sharp maneuvers to get ahead of an opponent.

Design & Trail

Sign icon: A sly racer cutting the inside line to outwit a rival — kakayêsimow, the tricky outwitter.

Wordplay / second meaning: The hustler's edge — outsmarting a rival on the loop rather than brute force.

Trail stats: Located in Gold Bar/Capilano Park; about 2 km long; -79 m descent / 84 m ascent.
