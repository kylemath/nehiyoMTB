About the Silver Fox

The carved plaque reads sôniyâwâhkêsis — 'silver little-fox', the everyday Plains Cree way to name the rare silver-coated colour-phase of the red fox. sôniyâw is the same word for 'money', so the compound carries a soft double-reading: a 'money-fox', the kind of fox whose pelt was once worth a small fortune in the fur trade. The diminutive ‑âhkêsis is the bound, vowel-reduced version of the free NA noun mahkêsîs 'fox'.

The plaque art picks up the same poise — a stylized white-and-black silhouette of a fox in a flowing, kitsune-like pose, head turned and tail curling forward. The carving is mostly black on white with red syllabics. The English label is rendered as SILVEREFOX on this particular plaque (extra E between SILVER and FOX) — likely a layout/carving quirk; the catalogue and most signage use the more standard Silverfox.

Silver Fox the trail is a short descent in the Kinsmen / Emily Murphy network in Northwest Edmonton, ridden as part of the popular 'Shredders Kinsmen Loop' alongside nikocwâsaskanôs / Six Shooter and sâpwâpahcikan pâskisikan / Ray Gun. It sits on ᓀᐦᐃᔭᐤ ᐊᐢᑭᕀ Nêhiyaw‑Askiy (Plains Cree territory).
