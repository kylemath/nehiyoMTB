Machete (mistâpiskihkomân) mesh-repair comparison

Each viewer below renders the same source STL after a different repair / solidify strategy. Cameras and lighting are identical; orbit one viewer and they all rotate together. Look at the cutouts and engraved detail to compare what each strategy keeps or fills.

Source: docs/mistapiskihkoman/mistâpiskihkomân_plaque_v3.stl · outputs live in mistapiskihkoman/_solid_compare/. Stats annotations are from admesh on each output.

The root cause: SketchUp emits 184 pairs of cancelling triangles (shared faces between abutting solid groups) and overlapping coplanar geometry that no automated topology repair can disambiguate without artistic intent. Option (8) voxel-rebuilds the visible surface on a regular grid and runs marching cubes; the result is manifold by construction and follows the original shape including any cutouts larger than the voxel pitch. The trade-off is voxel-grid stair-stepping on edges (visible at this 1 mm pitch; finer pitch costs RAM and time).
