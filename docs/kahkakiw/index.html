About the Raven

The raven (kahkakiw) holds significant cultural importance in many Indigenous traditions, including Plains Cree (nēhiyawēwin). Known for its intelligence, adaptability, problem-solving ability, and distinctive black plumage and rasping call, the raven appears in numerous oral stories and teachings.

In Cree storytelling the raven is often a trickster and messenger figure — a bird that watches, remembers, and carries news between worlds. This trail sign honours that presence with a stylized black raven silhouette and the syllabics ᑲᐦᑲᑭᐤ set across its body in red, so that the Cree name is literally carried on the raven itself.
