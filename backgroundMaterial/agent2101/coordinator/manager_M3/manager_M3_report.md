## Self-Assessment

**Craftsperson says:** The generated pages satisfy the structural contract from the exemplars and M2 report, preserve the hand-authored pages, resolve STL links from actual `docs/{folder}` files, and avoid broken image markup by using fallback design panels where assets are absent.

**Skeptic says:** STL selection is heuristic where `docsStlPath` was absent; several folders contain many historical or development STL variants, so a human may later prefer a different printable file. Some catalogue entries appear to carry English placeholders as `creeName` and missing syllabics, so those pages truthfully display pending-review status rather than richer language content.

**Mover says:** The target pages now exist and are complete enough for integration/link wiring. With more time, I would add curator-approved `docsStlPath`, images, Trailforks URLs, and reviewed related Cree forms to the catalogue so future regeneration can become less heuristic.
